Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is nationwide delivery?

Dhaka Metropolitan orders are dispatched with same/next day priority delivery. For all other districts across Bangladesh, standard delivery arrives in 2–3 business days.

What payment methods are supported?

We support Cash on Delivery (COD) nationwide, bKash & Nagad instant merchant transfers, as well as Visa, Mastercard, and American Express.

What is your return policy?

We provide a 7-day hassle-free return window. If any item is damaged, defective, or fails to meet your expectations, we arrange courier pickup for replacement or a 100% refund.
